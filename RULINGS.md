# Gameplay Rulings

## Kill-trigger growth

- Kill growth resolves only after damage and all resulting deaths in the exchange have resolved. The unit must still be alive at that point; growth cannot rescue an attacker that took lethal damage.
- Only the unit that initiated the exchange can trigger kill growth. A defender that kills an attacker through retaliation does not trigger it.
- Bristleback's surviving-damage growth can trigger at most once per round for each Bristleback.
- Leeching Tick's stats and identity remain unchanged in this pass; any identity retune is deferred to the balance wave.

Card text under `ui/seasons` remains unchanged pending issue #39.
