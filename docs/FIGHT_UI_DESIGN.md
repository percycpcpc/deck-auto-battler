# 2. Redesign concept (one direction, fully committed)

**The War Ledger.** A compact illuminated battle table, with two opposing warbands arranged beneath their command information. Bone health numerals and distinct incoming-card stations carry the strategy; painted characters and worn gold carry the atmosphere. The player watches the ledger change rather than searches a landscape for updates.

**Palette decisions.** Retain every ART_DIRECTION palette role and hex: background `#14101C`, panel `#1E1830`, raised `#2A2140`, bone `#EDE6D6`, secondary `#9A8FA8`, player `#46C2CB`, enemy `#D64550`, bronze `#B08D57`, silver `#C0C6D4`, tier gold `#E8B84B`, damage `#FF5A3C`, heal/buff `#7BE382`, shield `#FFD966`, poison `#6FCF3F`, UI gold `#E8C468`. Damage is a transient orange-red signal; allegiance remains teal/crimson even at low health. Small text never sits directly on either HP fill.

**Typography contract (CSS px; size / line height).** All numerals use tabular lining figures and zero tracking. No downloaded fonts.

# 6. Accessibility & readability floor

Use 4.5:1 minimum for all normal text, 3:1 for large text and meaningful outlines/icons against their adjacent backing. These are design acceptance thresholds, not a claim of audited compliance. Secondary `#9A8FA8` is for opaque background/panel text only; do not attenuate it again with opacity. Bone on opaque background/panel/raised plates is the default. Never put bone small text on a teal/crimson filled bar: HP numbers sit above it. Decorative dim ghost outlines may be below 3:1 because the explicit empty label supplies the information. Verify final composited contrasts, including badges and disabled controls, before shipping.

Absolute floors: hero HP24px, remaining counter24px, board stats22px, cost/deck16px, persistent names/labels/log14px. All text has an opaque backing if it crosses art. Show allegiance with `YOU`/`ENEMY` and fixed row position, low HP with text and border, damaged units with pip notch, buffs with `+`, shield with icon and text equivalent. Color alone never carries the state.

**Under-one-second glance test:** freeze a representative fight for 1000ms, then ask the viewer for both hero HPs, both remaining charger delays (or empty), and which visible opposing unit has higher ATK/HP. Pass requires all three categories to be identified without hover, scrolling, or reading the log at desktop and 390?844. The design supports this through large isolated HP numerals, consistently located counter badges, aligned opposing columns, and identical stat-pip positions. This is a testable target, not a guarantee of reading all twenty units in a second. On mobile the test covers visible columns; offscreen strength is not silently summarized as a misleading total. At short heights where a hero is offscreen, the simultaneous two-HP test cannot pass; record the accepted scrolling compromise rather than claiming otherwise.

Use named regions for Enemy line and Your line. Hero bar exposes current/max numeric values; card accessible names include public name, ATK, HP, and keywords. Deck/charger hidden-state descriptions include only permitted information. Use a polite log announcement for committed meaningful events, not every tick; no live region on every stat. Focus ring: 2px UI gold plus 2px background separation. Passive cards do not masquerade as attack buttons; inspection controls say `Inspect ?圳. Expanded log is nonmodal, closes with Escape, and restores trigger focus. A scoped inspection line for Scout must be cleared when its reveal window expires. Respect reduced motion as specified above.
